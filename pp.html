Privacy Policy

ShePays Financial Services Private Limited

Last Updated: February 2026

At ShePays Financial Services Private Limited ("ShePays," "we," "us," or "our"), we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our digital banking services, mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We collect personal information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Full name, date of birth, and gender
  • Contact information (email address, phone number, mailing address)
  • Government-issued identification numbers (Aadhaar, PAN, passport, driver's license)
  • Financial information (bank account details, income information, credit history)
  • Employment information (employer name, job title, work address)
  • Biometric data (fingerprints, facial recognition data) for authentication purposes
  • Photos and documents for identity verification

1.2 Transaction Information

We collect information about your financial transactions, including:

  • Payment and transfer details
  • Account balances and transaction history
  • Merchant information and purchase details
  • Bill payment information
  • Investment and savings activity

1.3 Technical Information

We automatically collect technical information when you use our Services:

  • Device information (device type, operating system, unique device identifiers)
  • IP address and location data
  • Browser type and version
  • App usage data and analytics
  • Log files and crash reports
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

1.4 Third-Party Information

We may receive information about you from third parties, including:

  • Credit bureaus and financial institutions
  • Identity verification services
  • Government databases and public records
  • Social media platforms (with your consent)
  • Our business partners and affiliates

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

2.1 Service Provision

  • Opening and maintaining your accounts
  • Processing transactions and payments
  • Providing customer support
  • Delivering requested financial services
  • Managing your investments and savings

2.2 Security and Compliance

  • Verifying your identity and preventing fraud
  • Complying with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Conducting risk assessments and monitoring
  • Preventing money laundering and terrorist financing
  • Maintaining the security of our systems

2.3 Communication and Marketing

  • Sending service-related notifications
  • Providing account updates and statements
  • Marketing our products and services (with your consent)
  • Conducting surveys and research
  • Personalizing your experience

2.4 Analytics and Improvement

  • Analyzing usage patterns and trends
  • Improving our Services and developing new features
  • Conducting data analytics and research
  • Optimizing our technology infrastructure

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information in the following circumstances:

3.1 Service Providers

We share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in:

  • Payment processing and transaction settlement
  • Identity verification and fraud prevention
  • Cloud storage and data hosting
  • Customer support and communication
  • Marketing and analytics services

3.2 Legal and Regulatory Requirements

We may disclose your information when required by law or regulation, including:

  • Compliance with court orders and legal processes
  • Reporting to regulatory authorities (RBI, SEBI, FIU-IND)
  • Anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing obligations
  • Tax reporting requirements
  • Law enforcement investigations

3.3 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the acquiring entity, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

3.4 Consent-Based Sharing

We may share your information with third parties when you provide explicit consent, such as:

  • Connecting with other financial institutions
  • Integrating with third-party financial apps
  • Participating in loyalty or rewards programs
  • Accessing additional financial services

4. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your information:

4.1 Technical Safeguards

  • End-to-end encryption for data transmission
  • Advanced encryption standards (AES-256) for data storage
  • Multi-factor authentication and biometric security
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Secure cloud infrastructure with redundancy

4.2 Operational Safeguards

  • Access controls and role-based permissions
  • Employee background checks and security training
  • Incident response and breach notification procedures
  • Regular backup and disaster recovery testing
  • Compliance with industry security standards (ISO 27001, PCI DSS)

4.3 Monitoring and Detection

  • 24/7 security monitoring and threat detection
  • Fraud detection algorithms and machine learning
  • Real-time transaction monitoring
  • Automated security alerts and notifications

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our Services and maintain your accounts
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements
  • Prevent fraud and maintain security

Specific retention periods include:

  • Account information: 7 years after account closure
  • Transaction records: 10 years as required by RBI guidelines
  • Identity verification documents: 5 years after relationship ends
  • Marketing communications: Until you opt out or 3 years

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Request verification of data accuracy

  • Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal requirements)

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Authenticate your identity and maintain security
  • Analyze usage patterns and improve our Services
  • Provide personalized content and recommendations
  • Measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns

We may transfer your information to countries outside India for processing and storage. When we do so, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

Our Services are not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

Your continued use of our Services after the effective date of the updated Privacy Policy constitutes your acceptance of the changes.
